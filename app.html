1. Your creatives

The form starts with 8 made-up examples so you can click Analyze right away. Edit them, delete rows, or click "Clear all" to start fresh. Umbra remembers what you type in this browser, so refreshing the page will not lose it.

Import from a spreadsheet

Copy rows from Google Sheets or Excel (include the header row) and paste them in the box. Or pick a CSV file. Umbra matches your columns by their names, so the order does not matter. Columns it needs: Description, Format, Talking creator, Hook, Views, Clicks, Conversions, Spend. Optional: Creator name, Platform, Followers (or creator size), Length, Call to action, Post date, 3-second views, Avg watch time. Other columns are ignored.

 

See the template

Click "Copy template", paste it into cell A1 of a new Google Sheet or Excel file, and replace the example rows with your own.
