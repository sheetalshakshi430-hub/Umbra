Who it's for

Creator-marketing teams at agencies and brands who run campaigns with many creators and want to know why some posts worked, not just which ones did.

Your reporting tool already tells you reach, views and sales per creator. Umbra tells you what the best posts had in common: the hook, the format, the length, a talking creator or not, the call to action. Then it tells you how far to trust it.

How it works

1. Paste your resultsCopy rows from Google Sheets or Excel, or upload a CSV. Umbra reads your column names for you.
2. Pick what "winning" meansLowest cost per sale, most sales, best click rate, or most views.
3. Read the short answerWhat the winners shared, what held the losers back, and what to test next. Print it or copy it into an email.

Honest by design

"Across 8 creatives ranked by cost per conversion, the clearest difference is that creatives with a 'result first' hook did better. This is a hint, not a rule: only 2 creatives with it and 6 without, so luck could explain it."

Small batches create fake patterns. Umbra labels every finding as a hint, worth testing or a strong signal, shows how many posts it rests on, and warns you when two details always appeared together so it can't tell which one mattered.

Questions

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Umbra runs inside your browser and saves your work in that browser only. Nothing is uploaded to us. The one exception is the optional AI write-up: if you add your own Claude API key and click it, your creatives' details are sent to Anthropic to write the summary. See the privacy page.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Open Umbra and start. Because there are no accounts yet, your saved analyses live in one browser on one computer. Use Export backup in the Library to keep a copy.

How many posts do I need?

At least 4 to run it, 8 or more to see anything useful, and about 30 before patterns are worth acting on. Umbra tells you which of these you're at.

Does it watch my videos?

No. You describe each post (format, hook, length and so on) and Umbra compares those details against the results.

Can it pull data from Instagram or TikTok automatically?

Not yet. Most creator platforms and agencies can export a spreadsheet, and Umbra imports that. Automatic connections are on the roadmap.
